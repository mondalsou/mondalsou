# Portfolio molecular scenes

The page retains the original website copy and dark theme. Scientific illustrations
use fresh RDKit coordinates, generated by `build-molecular-scenes.py` with seed 2026,
and an independent Three.js viewer. No company molecular assets, results, artwork,
branding or scene implementations were included. Three.js is an MIT-licensed
third-party dependency; its licence is retained with the bundled module.

Rebuild: `/usr/bin/python3 scripts/build-molecular-scenes.py` (requires RDKit and NumPy).

- Lidocaine: three ETKDG/MMFF conformers with identical atoms and connectivity.
  Atom assembly is a visual introduction, not a reaction mechanism. Discrete
  conformers are shown separately rather than implying an MD trajectory.
- Aspirin packing: eight molecular copies in an illustrative periodic arrangement;
  not an experimentally observed or predicted polymorph.
- pKa: authored aspirin-to-water proton transfer in a transparent vessel. Water
  illustrates the process; this is not a nonaqueous pKa calculation.
- Property panel: explicit molecular solvent environment. No predicted values.
- Agent: illustrative acetanilide-to-paracetamol substitution. cLogP and TPSA are
  calculated locally with RDKit; they are descriptors, not evidence of potency,
  safety, permeability, or improved drug quality. No live LLM run is represented.

Viewer controls: pause/play, replay, mouse drag. Reduced-motion preferences pause
initial playback. Offscreen and hidden-document viewers do not render new frames.

## Subsequent visual refinement
- pKa retains the transfer but suppresses nonessential aspirin hydrogens and uses
  five background waters; the accepting water stays explicit and foregrounded.
- Property panel has selectable solvation, dissolution and octanol/water
  partitioning chapters. Motions are qualitative; no equilibrium populations or
  numerical solvation/solubility predictions are claimed.
- Agent panel now represents the agent with a robot, planning indicators and an
  editing arm. The shared scaffold is aligned by a proper Kabsch rotation; a
  para-H to OH edit is illustrated, followed by checking and refinement stages.
  The robot is a narrative device, not a live LLM or physical synthesis robot.
  Descriptor tables were removed from the agent panel.

## Focused logP replacement
The property panel now uses one transparent two-phase vessel: n-octanol above
water, neutral solute molecules, and illustrative bidirectional exchange. The
logP definition uses equilibrium concentrations. Drawn molecule counts and motion
are qualitative and must not be interpreted as a predicted partition coefficient.
The prior solvation/solubility chapter switcher has been removed.

## Permeability as the second view
The property card defaults to logP and offers a Permeability button. Its schematic
lipid bilayer has polar heads facing the aqueous compartments and paired tails
facing inward. Foreground lipids are omitted at the crossing solely as a viewing
cutaway, not as an aqueous pore. A neutral molecular example traverses the core;
this is an authored passive-diffusion illustration, not a calculated permeability
or a molecular-dynamics trajectory. Existing pause/replay controls apply to both.

## Readability pass
The four capability panels use a 2x2 grid (one column on narrow phones). Stage
labels are shortened and methodological notes are retained as viewer tooltips
rather than dense overlays. The pKa scene now has only two small background
waters, both below and behind the donor/acceptor; the accepting water and proton
remain prominent. Original website descriptions are unchanged.
